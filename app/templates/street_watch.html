Street Watch

Neighbors can ask you for footage through a link you send them. You pick a clip and approve it, or decline. They never see your cameras, and they can't tell whether you have footage until you answer.

  • One clip of up to 10 minutes from one camera. Its link ends after 24 hours, and you can end it sooner.
  • Shared clips have no sound unless you choose to include it.
  • A request covers up to 1 hour in the last 7 days. Each neighbor can have 3 waiting at once.
  • Times in your privacy schedule can't be shared. Requests and this log are deleted after 30 days.
  • Turning Street Watch off ends every request link and every clip link it made.
