Back up your settings

Saves alerts, hidden cameras, camera names, quality, connection choices and the privacy schedule to a file. Your Wyze login isn't included. Add a passphrase to lock the file so you can keep it anywhere, even in the cloud. Without the passphrase it can't be restored.

Download backup

Back up every day

Saves a locked copy once a day to a folder on this computer, such as a USB drive, a NAS share or a synced cloud folder. The last 7 days are kept. Leave the folder empty to stop.

Coming from docker-wyze-bridge?

Wyze Tunnel started as a copy of it, so your settings and stream links carry over as they are. Paste your docker-compose.yml or .env file and you get the lines to use here. Nothing is saved until you add them yourself.
